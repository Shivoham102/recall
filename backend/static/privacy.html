Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2025

Recall is a personal AI assistant desktop application. This policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What we collect

  • Gmail data: We read email metadata and content via the Google Gmail API on your behalf. This data is fetched on-demand and is not stored on our servers. No email content is permanently retained by Recall.
  • Google Calendar data: We read calendar events via the Google Calendar API to generate your morning brief. Calendar content is not stored server-side and is fetched fresh each time.
  • Voice audio: When you use voice input, your audio is recorded locally in the Tauri desktop app, streamed to our backend for transcription, and then discarded. Voice recordings are processed in-memory only and are never stored.
  • Recall items: Tasks, reminders, and notes you create are stored in our database (Supabase) associated with your account.
  • Proactive job results: Summaries and brief metadata generated by our scheduled jobs are stored temporarily in our database to enable delivery to your device.

2. How we use your data

We use your data solely to provide Recall's features:

  • Generating your daily morning brief (email summaries, calendar events, open tasks)
  • Enabling voice-based task and reminder creation
  • Delivering smart reminders and follow-up nudges
  • Learning your usage patterns to improve relevance over time

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising. It is never used to train AI models.

3. Third-party services

  • Google APIs (Gmail, Calendar): Data is fetched on-demand directly from Google's servers. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic API: Text is sent to Claude (Anthropic) for processing and generating responses. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Your recall items, session data, and proactive job results are stored in Supabase. Subject to Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Cartesia: Text is sent to Cartesia for voice synthesis. Subject to Cartesia's Privacy Policy.

4. Data retention

  • Email and calendar content: Never stored on our servers. Fetched live per request from Google's APIs.
  • Voice audio: Processed in-memory only. Discarded immediately after transcription. Not stored anywhere.
  • Recall items (tasks, reminders, notes): Retained until you delete them or request account deletion.
  • Proactive job results: Stored in our database. Deleted when your account is deleted.

5. Security

Your Google OAuth tokens are stored securely in our database and used only to fetch data on your behalf. We use HTTPS for all data transmission. Access is restricted to authenticated users only.

6. Your rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. To do so, email shivohamangal@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my account".

You can revoke Recall's access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be announced via the GitHub repository. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Questions or concerns? Contact Shivoham Angal at shivohamangal@gmail.com.
